# Trail logs of what happened with uima

## The initial field

:calendar: July 2026

It all started when Machina went down in July 2026. That was a good opportunity to start a multilayer field near me, with a potential cell score of 300 kMU.

I picked a couple of anchors for the baseline:

- [The Mourning Angel](https://intel.ingress.com/?pll=51.409637,-0.202108)
- [Willow Lane GR Postbox](https://intel.ingress.com/?pll=51.388283,-0.161835)

The spine was made up of 14 portals along the A24. I threw the field without any problems, so I kept it alive, intending to let the anchors decay and rethrow. With the double AP event coming up, it would make a good AP engine. I just needed a couple of SBULs, but I had enough stocked away for a while.

What I didn't know, however, was that the ENL agent `uima` had moved to the area in early August. He soon started destroying the anchor. I could then rethrow, and that was the end of it. You smash, I rethrow.

Then he started adding two pink shields. I still smashed and rethrew. It was easy AP; the only challenge was getting keys for the spine, but those were easy enough to get.

Then four pink shields started appearing, and the other agent involved was `play4BY`. I wondered whether they might be playing together, though I had no way to know.

He then started not only deploying four shields, but also flipping the portal blue, knowing full well that I wouldn't be able to rethrow the 14 links without a SoftBank Ultra Link mod. That was annoying, but luckily I could split the field between two anchors and avoid needing an SBUL at all. Most of the activity I saw was by `uima`, while `play4BY` added shields and upgraded the portal. I didn't see `play4BY` do anything that would show up in comms. That seemed unusual to me for a Level 8 player, who might also capture portals, throw links, and deploy items to build up AP.

And that's how we carried on for another month or so. Then, one morning, I went to farm keys. It was a bright, sunny morning, and I saw someone acting strangely between the two portals. Then the portals went down, smashed by `uima`. I was curious, so I waited to see what would happen next. Sure enough, `play4BY` started appearing on both anchors, with four shields.

At that point, I suspected that one person was using two accounts. The Resistance agent Riggs97 and Jeannie told me they believed the same person had regularly smashed Riggs97's home portal and Jeannie's nearby deskies.

Then the ENL agent `PastaTrolls` moved back here. He started smashing the spine, moving towards my home area and home portals. At the same time, `uima` started smashing my home area from the other end, also moving towards my home portals. I flipped them, and they both went away.

However, with the spine smashed, `__MACHINA__` spread in relatively quickly, and that field was history.

Days later, agent `uima` came back to smash my hack-modded homie. Annoying, but nothing dramatic. That was enough for me to decide to go and smash his own area, then put links through it so he couldn't rethrow his fields. One end was at the end of the spine; the other was in a quiet part of Wimbledon, perhaps a 25-minute walk from what I think is his home area. He went to smash the blockers so he could rethrow his fields, but I got back and rethrew the block before he could do anything. This is when agent `bysekin` appeared out of nowhere, smashed the portal, didn't capture anything around it, and then disappeared. I thought that was a bit odd. Agent `uima` couldn't rethrow his fields.

I noticed that agents `bysekin` and `uima` shared a banner from Minsk. I wondered whether they knew each other, but the shared banner alone doesn't tell me that.

## The flipped field

:calendar: October 2026

I still had all the keys for the spine, so my next idea was to move the baseline south and build a field going north instead. That would mean less AP, but I could potentially add another 10 portals to the spine relatively easily without affecting other local RES agents. The good thing was that it wouldn't affect `uima` and his _friends_.

Agent `uima` went to smash agent `Riggs97`'s and Jeannie's homies. As a good local citizen, I went to help rebuild, and that was it. I may have smashed fields that `uima` had created, just normal play for AP and tasks. Anyway, at some point `uima` came to smash my homie. Just the home portal, nothing else. Targeted attack?

As usual, I went to smash his home area and threw a five-layer field over it. I think I destroyed all the portals with his name on them. `uima` rebuilt his area, so I went back to smash it and rethrow the field over his head. Obviously, `play4BY` came back into play, and we started seeing portals with four Aegis shields. My Ultra Strikes love the hissing sound of a dying Aegis shield. That's a nice challenge to have. Besides, with the double 8 and double AP events coming up, that was going to be juicy AP.

## The 4th Agent

:calendar: 14 November 2026

As soon as double AP started, a new ENL agent, `Komsa007`, appeared in `uima`'s playing area. It was pretty obvious this wasn't a newbie. They knew how to glyph, link, and field. With double AP in play, it took them a day to grind to Level 8. What we then noticed was that all the portals in `uima`'s area were Level 8, with two Level 8 resonators from each of `uima`, `play4BY`, `bysekin`, and, of course, `Komsa007`. How strange. We noticed that `uima` and `play4BY` deployed together first, followed by `Komsa007` and `bysekin` deploying together.

Needless to say, those portals got smashed the next day and re-fielded. I then started smashing after `uima` and `play4BY` deployed. I ended up catching up with him at what we can consider his home portal, where both `uima` and `play4BY` captured it and deployed pink shields, or Aegis. I was just using US8s to pop the shields. It lasted about eight minutes, proper anomaly style... except there was only one other person visible, the same as a few months ago, and they totally ignored any invitation to come and say hi. I didn't win the portal, but I didn't expect to. A single agent can't outgun two agents at the same time... Agents? Or should I say a dual-accounting agent playing with two phones?

I suppose he was hoping to drain me of guns, but that didn't happen. My hack-modded homie gave me enough guns, and the next day it was all back to blue, with five layers over his head.

## The home flip under his nose...

:calendar: 19 November 2026

With double 8, it was easy for me to get back to Level 8 in a day or so after it was smashed. Besides, `uima`'s work pattern was pretty easy to guess. So I didn't bother with shields. I put two VRMHs, VRHSs, and an ITO- on the portal, knowing full well he would come and smash it. But I was ready, and as soon as he was around the corner, I flipped it. I flipped both homies, even though I couldn't reach them. They're an extra 30-minute walk from him. It was dark, cold, and rainy... That must have been very disappointing.

Nevertheless, he came back the next morning before going to work, and yes, I was still asleep. He got the homie and pinapled it, then smashed the other one, not realising he'd smashed the pineapple he'd just made... except for three resonators and one mod. But they were going to decay within two days. Nothing dramatic. I just have to make sure no one recharges it.

## The 5th Agent

:calendar: 21 November 2026

But the day after, it was back to fully charged. I couldn't believe it. What was even odder was that a new RES agent, `TGXHere`, deployed another two mods on each of the two portals. RES agent `TGXHere`, Level 2, had only deployed four mods in their entire career, and they both appeared on my homies? What are the odds that this is another `uima` account?
